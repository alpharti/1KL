war thunder is a very "relaxing" game it is grindy but worthy. you can easily get cool skins from warthunder live. and the game devs support it a lot and make tutorials on installing and making skins. my favorite vehicle is jagtiger rn.

roblox is a great game for creativity and fun. you can make your own games and play games made by other users.

my favorite games on the platform are NiC and TDX because i like military and strategy

ace combat 7 is a flight sim game with a great story and amazing graphics. the gameplay is fun and engaging. my favorite plane is the X-02S Strike Wythren.

the multiplayer lobbies are sweaty but the campaign will prepare you .

rainbow six siege is a tactical fps game that requires teamwork and strategy. my favorite operator is deimos cuz i aint scared and because of his tracking,his revolver can deliver nasty headshots.

the game has a steep learning curve but is very rewarding once you get the hang of it.
